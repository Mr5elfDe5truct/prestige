      Knowledge
      
          
      Chat with your files. Prestige reads them on this PC and answers with the file and page it found each fact on. Nothing leaves this computer.
      
        
      
        
      
      
      
      
      
      
        
        Drop PDFs, Word documents, notes or a whole folder here
        
      
      
      
      
      Your files
      
      
      
      
      Reads PDF, Word (.docx), text, Markdown, HTML, CSV, JSON and code. With the Read documents pack it also reads scanned PDFs and photos or scans of pages (pick them with Add files…). Files added from a folder or the picker are read where they are; dropped files are copied into Prestige's app data folder. Removing a file here never deletes your original.

      Teach a face or style
      
          
      Give Prestige 10 to 30 photos of someone (or a character, a pet, a thing), or of a look, and it learns them: then Qwen-Image can put that face or style in any picture. It trains on this PC with Fizgig.
      
        
      
        
      
      
      
      
        Training needs Fizgig and the full Qwen-Image-2.1 model it trains on: about 57 GB to download, once.
        
      
      
      
      
        
      
          
          
          
        
      
        
      
        
      
        
      
        
      Mix close-ups with wider shots, and different light and clothes; for a style, pictures of different things in that style. Only use photos of real people with their permission.
