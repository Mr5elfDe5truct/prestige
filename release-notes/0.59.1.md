## Prestige 0.59.1 · Swap panel

**New**
- **The Swap panel**: a place to put a video and a picture of who goes in it.
  - In Studio's **Film** tab, click the **⇆ Swap** chip next to *Short film*. In **Video** mode, click **⇆ Swap a character**.
  - Two big boxes open in the Create area: **Video** on the left, **Person** on the right.
  - Fill each one by dropping a file from Explorer or a render from the Renders gallery, by pasting, or by clicking the box to choose a file. **Or a character** puts a saved Character's face in the Person box.
  - Each box shows what's in it (the video plays, muted, when you point at it). **×** clears a box.
- Under the boxes: who to replace (*person* unless you say), Draft or Standard, how long it will take, and a big **Swap** button.
  - The swap goes into the render queue like any render, with the chat model unloaded first and the video's own sound kept. You can set up the next one while it runs.
  - The result lands in **Renders**. **Add to "film"** puts it at the end of your open (or latest) film as a new shot.
- Right-click a video → **Swap a character…** now opens the panel with that video in it. Dropping a gallery video on the Create area does the same.

**Measured on an RTX 3060 12 GB**
- A 3.4 s 768×512 clip at Draft, from the Film tab: 8 min 53 s (estimated 9 min).
