## Prestige 0.53.0 · Uncensored renders

**New**
- **Uncensored video by default**: Animate, long video and video from a picture now run an uncensored Wan 2.2 pair (such as Dasiwa SynthSeduction) when one is in ComfyUI's model folders. The model picks have Auto (uncensored if installed), Stock, or any file.
- **Video from a picture runs on Wan 2.2**: no sound, uncensored. LTX-2.5 with sound is still a choice under Model in the Video settings. Storyboard shots stay on LTX.

**Fixed**
- **Chat kept on your model when you attach a picture**: Qwen3.8 27B can see pictures, so Prestige no longer swaps to Gemma 4, which refused. When a switch is needed, it prefers an uncensored model that can see.

**Good to know**
- Photos brought in from outside (picked, pasted, attached, webcam, phone), and renders made from them, keep the standard models.
- Edits need an instruction: "Nude" alone leaves the picture as it is; "Remove her clothes, fully nude, same pose" works.
