## Prestige 0.57.0 · Faster pictures, worlds to explore and two-card chat

**Faster**
- **Pictures and edits about 2.5× faster.** Qwen-Image-2.1 now runs from Comfy-Org's int8 files, with the uncensored LoRA on top. On an RTX 3060:
  - a 20-step edit takes 40 s instead of 102 s;
  - a Fast edit takes 12 s instead of 27 s;
  - a Turbo picture takes 12 s instead of 21 s, now on Qwen's own 8-step Turbo.
  The older GGUF models still run if the new files aren't there yet.
- **Faster video** (a new setting, on by default): Wan 2.2 runs with SageAttention on RTX 30-series and newer cards. A 4-shot long video takes 25 min instead of 29.5. It does nothing on older cards or where it isn't installed.
- **Chat models across two cards.** With two GPUs and **llama.cpp --fit** on Auto (Workstation settings), llama.cpp spreads the big models over both cards instead of keeping one. On two RTX 3060s:
  - Qwen3.6 35B goes from 28 to 53 tok/s;
  - GLM-4.7 Flash goes from 20 to 73 tok/s;
  - Qwen3-VL 30B goes from 16 to 68 tok/s;
  - Hy-Memory goes from 19 to 87 tok/s;
  - prompts are read 2–9× faster.

**New**
- **Explore a world** (`/world` in chat): Tencent's Hy-Nomas makes a world 5 seconds at a time.
  - `/world a fox walking through a misty forest` starts one. Attach a picture to start from it.
  - `/world then the fox stops and sniffs the flowers` continues the same world.
  - `/world then camera: W 2, W+Left 1.5` steers the camera with keys (W/A/S/D move, arrows turn, seconds each).
  - Each reply is the whole world so far as one video. On an RTX 3060 at 832×480 it takes about 1 min 45 s per segment, up to 4 segments.
- **Edits on MOSS-Image**: set **Edit with** to MOSS-Image in the image settings, and Edit, Paint to change, layout edits, Reference and `/edit` use it, at about 13 s Fast. An edit can also take a second picture:
  - in Studio, from the Reference slot, which now stays open while you edit;
  - in chat, up to three attached pictures ("put the hat from image 2 on her"). This works with Qwen-Image too.
- **See-through pictures**: set the image settings' **Background** to **Transparent** for one subject on nothing (a sticker, a sprite, a logo). You get a PNG with real transparency, glass and chrome included, in about 12 s.
- **Redraw at 4 MP / 8 MP** (right-click a picture, next to Upscale): Qwen-Image with the Restore LoRA redraws the picture a tile at a time, adding real detail instead of sharpening. From 1 MP it takes about 1 min to 4 MP on Fast, or 5 min at Quality.

**New packs and pack changes**
- **Explore a world** (Hy-Nomas, 26.7 GB). Restart the services afterwards.
- **Images** now brings the int8 Qwen-Image files and the uncensored LoRA (41.9 GB in all).
- **MOSS-Image** now brings the edit LoRA, the Alpha LoRA and the RGBA VAE (14.9 GB).
- **Reshoot** now brings ausboss's tiled-upscale nodes.

**Fixed**
- Picking Kromoss in MOSS-Image's settings asked ComfyUI for a file that didn't exist.
- Video's *From a picture* choice (Wan or LTX) is now remembered after a restart.

**Good to know**
- **MOSS edits need a ComfyUI newer than 0.42.5** (its current main branch). Until then a MOSS edit says so; Qwen-Image edits are unaffected.
