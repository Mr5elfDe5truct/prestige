## Prestige 0.60.0 · See what it's doing

**New**
- **Working readout** in the top bar, on every screen. While Prestige works, the top hat appears with its red eye glowing and a gold ring turning round it, and next to it:
  - **who** is doing it: the chat model, Do it for me's vision model, or a mission's model;
  - the **tool** it's calling right now (web_search, read, make_image, search_my_files, a Do it for me click…), in a red tag. The ring turns red and spins faster while a tool runs;
  - **what's happening**, in words: Thinking, Searching the web for "…", Reading reddit.com, Waiting for you to allow it, Reading the results, Writing the reply, Rendering (42%);
  - how long it's been going, and a bar along the bottom (how far when it's known, a gold glint when it isn't).
- It follows **chat replies, tool calls, Deep Research, Do it for me, Missions** (which run in the background) and the **render queue**. When more than one is running it says **+2**; click it for a list of every job with its model, tool, step, time and progress.
- In the chat, the step a reply is on now **shimmers gold** behind a small turning eye, instead of plain grey text.
- **Tool steps** in a reply say which model called them ("by Qwen3.8-27B"), and the one that's running glows gold until it's done.
- The line under the top bar glows along while anything is working, and "● generating" under the message box is now three bouncing dots.

**Good to know**
- Animations stop when Windows has "Show animations" turned off, as everywhere else in Prestige.
