## Prestige 0.60.4 · Steadier cloned voices

**Fixed**
- A cloned voice could speed up, slow down, get louder or quieter, or drop to a whisper from one sentence to the next. A clone saved without a transcript was cloned from the sound alone, and VoxCPM2 is unsteady that way. Prestige now writes down what the clip says (with Whisper) and clones from the clip and its words together. Voices you already cloned get this the next time they speak, and the original recording is kept beside them as `<name>.original.flac`.
- Voice clips are cleaned when they're added: silence at the ends and long pauses are cut, the clip is brought to one steady level, and a long one is cut to about 20 seconds at the end of a sentence.
- Every spoken sentence is brought to the same loudness.
- VoxCPM2 now uses 10 steps in chat and on the Voice screen (was 6) and 6 in Live (was 4). Fewer steps let a cloned voice wander. Live replies take a little longer to start.
