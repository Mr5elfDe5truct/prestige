## Prestige 0.50.0 · Everything in one app

The Custom AI Workstation now lives inside Prestige. One installer, and Prestige sets up, runs and updates the whole AI stack itself.

**New**
- **Setup**: on a PC without a Workstation, Setup opens by itself. Pick a folder and the model packs (the ones that suit your PC are ticked) and it installs the tools, llama.cpp for your driver, the Python environments, ComfyUI and the packs, showing each step. Run it again any time to repair (Settings → Set up or repair the Workstation).
- **Packs** (System → Packs): every model pack with what's already on this PC and whether it fits your cards. Get with progress, Pause, Resume where it stopped, and Remove (files other packs still use are kept). **Engines**: update llama.cpp or go back one build, update ComfyUI and Ollama.
- **Memory of its own**: Prestige keeps what models know about you on this PC. Say "remember that …" in any chat, or open Memory (the line under the chat box) to add, find, edit or delete facts. Open WebUI's memory is copied in when it's connected.
- **No more PowerShell**: Prestige works out the GPU plan, writes the config files and starts every service itself, and closing it stops everything in about two seconds.
- **Open WebUI is optional**: a pack like any other, off on new installs, and nothing waits for it when it isn't there.

**Better**
- Every service now numbers your graphics cards the same way, so nothing lands on the wrong card.
- The Workstation's files ship with each release and stay in step with it; your own llama-models.ini is never overwritten.

Already have a Workstation? Nothing to do: Prestige finds it and updates its files on the first start.
