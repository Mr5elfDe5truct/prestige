## Prestige 0.56.1 · Layout boxes on your pictures

**Better**
- **▦ Layout with a picture**: the layout boxes now work with a picture you've dropped on Studio, not only for MOSS-Image from text.
  - **Edit**: the boxes are drawn on the picture. Only the boxes change, each to what you write in it, and the rest stays exactly as it was. The prompt is optional. The Speed setting (Fast or Quality) applies.
  - **Reference**: mark the box your reference goes in (*Your reference*), and add boxes for other things, people and text. MOSS-Image lays out the scene, then Qwen-Image puts your reference into its box, keeping its likeness.
  - **Animate**: boxes can't steer an animation, so the Layout button is shown greyed out and says why. Lay out the picture with Edit first, then animate the result.
- An edit's boxes are kept apart from a new picture's, and they're cleared when you edit a different picture.
