## Prestige 0.56.0 · Faster edits, a second picture model and audio dramas

**New**
- **Fast edits**: edits, Paint to change, Restore photo and chat's `/edit` have a **Speed** setting. **Fast** (the default) renders in 4 steps with the Lightning LoRA instead of 20–25, and Reference's **Turbo** uses it too. **Quality** keeps the full steps.
- **Restore photo** (right-click a picture): a small, compressed, blurry or worn photo is redrawn at about 2 megapixels, keeping the people and the framing. Pick *small or compressed*, *blurry* or *an old print*.
- **Make it manga** (right-click a picture): the picture becomes an inked black-and-white manga panel with real screentone.
- **MOSS-Image**: a second picture model for text to image. Click Studio's model chip until it says MOSS-Image; chat's `/image` uses the same pick. It's good with lettering. **▦ Layout** lets you drag boxes for where text, things and people go. **Kromoss** (more natural people) can be picked in its settings.
- **Cut out a person** (right-click a video): click the person on the first frame, or type *the woman*. You get a transparent WebM, PNG frames, a green-screen MP4, or the person on a new background picture or video. Hair stays in, and the sound is kept.
- **Learn from chats** (in Memory): once a chat has been quiet for a moment, a memory writer adds, updates or removes facts about you. It can be the chat model, or Hy-Memory, a model made for exactly this. A new message stops it, and it's off until you turn it on.
- **Audio dramas and podcasts**: `/drama` or `/podcast` and an idea. The chat model writes a short script for up to 16 voices, with cues like *[laughs]* and *[whispering]*. You give each character one of your saved voices (or Drama 3's own) and can change the script. Fish Audio's Drama 3 performs it, with an optional music bed, as one MP3.
- **Talk over it in Live calls**: talking over Prestige lowers its voice at once. Keep going and it stops and listens; a "mm-hm", "yeah" or "right" lets it carry on. With the Turn-taking pack, TYGER tells a real interruption from a backchannel and waits through your pauses until you've finished.

**New packs**
- **Images** now includes the Lightning LoRA (67 MB). **Reshoot** now includes the Restore LoRA (0.7 GB in all).
- **MOSS-Image** (14.2 GB) and **Kromoss** (6.6 GB).
- **Video matting** (MatAnyone2, 0.3 GB): its ComfyUI nodes are installed for you. Restart the services afterwards.
- **Hy-Memory** (18.2 GB), **Audio drama** (Drama 3 and audio.cpp, 17.9 GB) and **Turn-taking** (TYGER, 2.6 GB; needs Voice).

**Good to know**
- Measured on an RTX 3060 12 GB with 32 GB of RAM:
  - A Fast edit takes about 30 s instead of about 105 s.
  - Restore photo takes about 1.5 min Fast, or about 4.5 min at Quality.
  - Make it manga takes about 1.5 min Fast.
  - A MOSS-Image picture takes about 15 s (8 GB of GPU memory).
  - Cutting a person out of an 8 s clip takes about 25 s, plus about 15 s for the WebM.
  - Learn from chats with Hy-Memory takes about 15–30 s after a chat (10.4 GB).
  - Drama 3 needs about 5 s of work per second of speech: a 24 s scene took 2 min, about 4.5 min with the music bed, using 10–11.4 GB of GPU memory and about 10 GB of RAM.
  - TYGER checks every 320 ms in about 55 ms, using about 4.3 GB.
- **MOSS-Image needs ComfyUI 0.42 or newer.** Update it in Comfy Desktop first; until then a MOSS render says so.
- **MatAnyone2** (Cut out a person) is free for **non-commercial use only** (NTU S-Lab License 1.0). Only cut out people with their permission.
- **Drama 3** is under the Fish Audio Community License: free below US$500k a year of revenue. Only clone a real person's voice with their consent, and don't remove anything that marks the audio as generated.
- New Camera angle and Try on outfit keep their full steps. Lightning stopped the camera turning and let the outfit swap move the person.
- TYGER loads when the voice card has room for it: with a Kokoro voice, not next to VoxCPM2. Without it, Live still decides from your words. English only.
