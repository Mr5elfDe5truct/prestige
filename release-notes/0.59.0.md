## Prestige 0.59.0 · Film

**New**
- **Film**: a new tab in Studio, next to Storyboard, for making a short film or a music video the way an editor works. Type an idea and click **New film** (or leave it empty to start blank), or open one of your films from the list under the prompt. Each film is saved on its own, and you can rename or delete it.
  - **1 · Cast**: add your Characters (their face and voice), or any picture as a cast member with a name. Set each one's voice and how they look.
  - **2 · Plan**: the chat model writes it. A **short film** (30 s to 3 min) gets its shots, who's in each, what happens and what's said. A **music video** gets the Director's song and 5 s shots, and **Make the song** sings it with ACE-Step. You can change any of it.
  - **3 · Shots**: one card per shot, 2 to 10 s each.
    - **Draw frame**: Qwen-Image puts the cast member from their picture into the scene.
    - **Shoot / Retake**: LTX-2.5 with sound, from the frame, with a new seed each time.
    - **Long take**: Wan 2.2 SVI, from the frame.
    - **Swap…**: puts a cast member into the take, or into a clip of your own (SCAIL-2).
    - **Upscale**: 1080p with SeedVR2.
    - **Sound…**: LTX-2.3 Foley.
    - **Lip-sync**: InfiniteTalk makes a close-up of the speaker saying the shot's lines.
    - Every result is kept as a take; click one to use it, or drop it.
  - **4 · Timeline**: the takes in shot order, plus title cards.
    - Trim each clip, cut or crossfade into it, and set how loud its own sound is. Drag a clip to move it.
    - Dialogue lines are spoken in each cast member's voice (Kokoro or VoxCPM2) and laid over the shots. Add a music bed (the film's song, or one from the PC) and set its level.
    - **Send back** opens a clip's shot; a new take there lands in the same place on the timeline.
    - **Export MP4** cuts it all together with ffmpeg and puts it in the gallery.
  - Every render goes through the render queue one at a time, with the chat model unloaded first, as the Director does. Each shot's card shows how far along its render is.

**Measured on an RTX 3060 12 GB**
- A test film, "The Last Letter": 3 shots (6, 8 and 6 s) with one cast member from a picture, a swapped clip, a title card and one line of dialogue.
  - Planning it with Qwen3.6 35B: about 4 min, loading the model included.
  - The three frames with the cast member in them: about 5 min together.
  - Each shot at Standard, from its frame: 8 to 9 min (6 s shots about 8 min, the 8 s one about 9 min). All three shots, frames included, took 30 min.
  - The swap at Draft (6 s): about 14 min. A second run took 43 min while system RAM was nearly full (SCAIL-2 needs about 25 GB).
  - Voicing the line (Kokoro) and exporting the 21.6 s film: a few seconds each.

**Good to know**
- There's no **Extend** yet: none of the workflows carries a clip on past its end.
- Lines are spoken over the shot; only **Lip-sync** moves the speaker's lips. A shot with lines plays its own LTX sound quietly (25%), since LTX's made-up speech isn't the cast's. Raise it on the timeline if you want it louder.
- Only use photos of real people with their permission, and never make sexual content of anyone under 18.
