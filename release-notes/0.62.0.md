## Prestige 0.62.0 · Quick talk, whole documents, splats

**New**
- **Quick talk.** Live calls have a **Mode** picker: **Full** (as before, the default) or **Quick**. In Quick, one small model, Liquid AI's LFM3-Audio 2B, hears you and answers aloud, with no Whisper, chat model or separate voice. It starts answering about 1 s after you stop talking (0.4 s of that is the model) and uses ~2.4 GB of VRAM. It has one voice, speaks English only, doesn't think much, can't see the camera, and Quick calls aren't saved to Past chats. It needs the new **Quick talk** pack (1.4 GB) and llama.cpp b11960 (v0.8.0) or newer: **Packs → Engines → Update**. LFM3-Audio is under the LFM Open License v1.0, free for companies under $10M a year in revenue.
- **Read whole documents.** The new **Read whole documents** pack adds Baidu's Unlimited-OCR-2. Knowledge reads the scanned pages of a PDF with it all together, and **Read the text** gets a **Read again, carefully** button. It's far better on a full page of text: on a dense test page it got 98.7% of the words, where the first one got stuck repeating itself. But it's slower: about 20 s for a short page and 90 s for a full one on an RTX 3060, with ~7.3 GB of VRAM. The first reader stays the default and the fallback.
- **Picture to 3D as a splat.** Right-click a picture → **Make a 3D splat**: Speridlabs' Idol turns it into a photo-real 3D Gaussian splat, the sides it never saw included, in about 2 minutes on an RTX 3060 (~6.2 GB of VRAM). It lands with your 3D models and turns in the Canvas's 3D viewer. It works best on one object. It needs the **Picture to 3D · splat** pack and a Hugging Face token whose account has accepted the DINOv3 and FLUX.2-dev licences. FLUX.2-dev's VAE, which it uses, is non-commercial. The first one downloads 6.4 GB more.

**Good to know**
- The two new Python packs (Read whole documents and Picture to 3D · splat) are large: 10.5 and 11.5 GB.
- Pull request checks now also run the Rust tests.
