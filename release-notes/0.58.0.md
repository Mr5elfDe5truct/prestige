## Prestige 0.58.0 · Swap a character

**New**
- **Swap a character**: put the character from a picture in place of a person in a video. They move as the person did; everyone else, the background and the sound stay as they were.
  - Right-click any video → **Swap a character…**, or click **⇆ Swap a character** in Studio's Video mode. That uses the video selected in the gallery, or asks you to pick one from the PC.
  - **Who goes in**: one of your Characters' faces, or any picture you choose, paste or drop on the window (a render from the gallery works too). A full-body picture on a plain background works best.
  - **Who to replace**: *person* unless you say otherwise, e.g. *the woman in red*. If several people match, the biggest one is replaced.
  - **Draft** (4 steps) or **Standard** (8 steps). The window shows how long it will take before you start.
  - It runs on Z.ai's SCAIL-2 (Wan 2.1 14B, int8) with its DPO and lightx2v LoRAs. SAM 3.1 finds the person; BiRefNet cuts the character out of the picture.
  - It works 3.4 s at a time, and each part carries on from the one before with its colours matched. The result keeps the video's own frame rate. Videos up to 20 s; a longer one has only its first 20 s swapped.
  - On an RTX 3060 a 5 s clip takes about 12 min at Draft (about 18 min at Standard), using up to 10.9 GB of VRAM and about 25 GB of system RAM. It goes through the render queue like any other render, and the chat model is unloaded first.

**New pack**
- **Swap a character** (29.1 GB; 19.1 GB new if you have the Talking and Select packs). Needs ComfyUI 0.42 or newer.

**Good to know**
- Every 3.4 s part reads the 16.7 GB model again. From a hard drive that takes about 2 min 15 s per part; from an SSD, about 40 s. The model sits in its own folder (`models\comfy\diffusion_models\scail2`), which can be a junction to an SSD.
