## Prestige 0.52.0 · Use the whole screen

Studio and System now fill the window, in panels you can move and resize.

**New**
- **Panels that fill the window**: Studio is Create, Render queue and Renders side by side; System is GPUs, Models, What's in VRAM and Services. Each panel scrolls on its own, so the Create bar stays put while you browse renders.
- **Resize**: drag the line between two panels or two columns. Double-click a line to even them out.
- **Move**: drag a panel by its title bar. Drop it above or below another panel, or on another panel's left or right edge to give it a column of its own.
- **Kept and reset**: your layout is kept for next time. The ⟲ button on any panel's title bar puts that screen back the way it came.
- **Wider chat**: drag either side of the Chat column to make it wider or narrower. Double-click a side for the usual width.

Small windows still stack the panels in one column.
