## Prestige 0.51.0 · Eight new tools

Eight new tools, each in its own pack (System → Packs), so you only download the ones you want.

**New**
- **Reshoot and dress up**: right-click a picture → **New camera angle…** to see the same scene from another side, height or closer in, or **Try on outfit…** to put the person in clothes from another picture. Qwen-Image 2.1 with the Multiple-Angles and Outfit Swap LoRAs, about 2–3 minutes a picture on an RTX 3060.
- **Search everything**: the magnifier at the top (or Ctrl+Shift+F) finds renders, songs, chats and files by meaning, in your own words. Pictures are found by what they show, even without a prompt. EmbeddingGemma 2 (needs llama.cpp b11452 or newer).
- **Read any document**: right-click a picture → **Read the text** turns a photo or scan into text with its headings and tables, ready to copy. Knowledge now reads scanned PDFs and pictures too. Unlimited-OCR, about 1.5 seconds a page.
- **Make it sound human**: the pen beside a reply, or under the chat box, rewrites AI-sounding text so it reads like a person wrote it, facts kept. Ctrl+Z puts your own text back.
- **Translate**: the globe under the chat box translates into any of 150 languages, and Studio's **Translate the text…** reads and translates signs, menus and screenshots. Index-Translate 35B-A3B, its experts kept in RAM so it fits beside a 12 GB card.
- **Teach it a face or style**: Studio → Image → **✦ Teach a face or style**. Give it 10–30 photos of someone, or of a look, and it trains a LoRA on your PC (Fizgig). Pick it in **Your LoRA** and it shows up in new renders. About 75 minutes for 20 photos on an RTX 3060.
- **Sprite sheets for characters**: a character's **Make sprites…** draws them full-body in 4 poses (or all of them) on a transparent background, and puts the sprites on one sheet. VNCCS, in a ComfyUI of its own.
- **Sound for silent videos**: right-click a video → **Add sound…**, describe it, and LTX-2.3 with Lightricks' Foley LoRA makes sound effects that follow the picture. The video is saved again with its sound. About 18 minutes for each 4 seconds.

**Better**
- **Gated models**: Packs has a place for a Hugging Face token, for packs whose model needs a licence accepted first. Your hf auth login works too.
