## Prestige 0.54.0 · Design a voice

**New**
- **Design a voice from words**: in a Character's editor (**Design one…** beside the voice) or on the Voice screen (**Design a voice…**), describe it, e.g. "a gravelly old pirate, slow and amused", and VoxCPM2 makes 2 or 3 takes to listen to. Keep the one you like: it's saved like a cloned voice, so it sounds the same in every reply, call and talking video, and a character's is picked for them straight away.

**Good to know**
- On an RTX 3060, 3 takes take about 45 s (about 90 s when VoxCPM2 has to load first) and use 6.2 GB of GPU memory; the chat model steps aside while it works.
- Each take comes out different, even with the same words. Not happy with any? Change the words a little and make more.
