## Prestige 0.60.1 · Fixes for the working readout and early cut-offs

**Fixed**
- While Prestige was working (thinking, writing, running a tool, rendering), the whole window shrank into a narrow column on the left. A stray glowing outline also floated across the chat, and the top bar could cover your message. 0.60.0's readout styling was being applied to the whole page while it worked, not just to the readout. Now only the readout in the top bar is styled.
- The same mix-up could also restyle "Make it sound human" while it rewrote a reply, and the search bar's credit line while it searched. Both look as they did before 0.60.0 again.
- Replies could stop early with "this reply filled the 20k context" after only a few thousand tokens. Most of the context was going to tool descriptions sent with every message (all tool groups together are about 20k tokens). Tool groups now take at most a third of a model's context: the biggest ones are left out for that reply, and a note says which.
- The cut-off note now says how much of the context went to the chat and tools, and how much was left for the reply.

**Changed**
- With llama.cpp on two cards (22 GB or more), Qwen3.8 27B Uncensored gets a 128k context instead of 20k. It still fits fully on two RTX 3060s at 22-26 tok/s.
